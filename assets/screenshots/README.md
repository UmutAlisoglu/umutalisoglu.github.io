# Project screenshots

`skate-scape.jpg` is an actual browser capture of Skate Scape in Lumbridge, taken on 22 October 2026. It shows the unofficial fan project running at `https://alisoglu.com/skate-scape/`. RuneScape and its game imagery belong to Jagex Ltd; this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jagex. See the [game’s notices](https://github.com/UmutAlisoglu/skate-scape/blob/main/NOTICE.md).

`skate-scape-session.jpg` is a second actual gameplay capture, taken on 23 October 2026 after moving and jumping in Lumbridge. It is used on the homepage project card so that the hero and project list show different views. The same RuneScape attribution and unofficial fan-project notice above apply.

`fileforge.jpg` is an actual capture of FileForge’s connected browser workspace (PDF organizer) using the original, locally generated three-page PDF sample, retaken on 27 November 2026 after the blue/white redesign. It contains no visitor documents or private files.

The Repodock dashboard image at `../repodock-dashboard.png` comes from that project’s public documentation. These screenshots show the projects themselves; they are not mockups or personal photographs.

`pcb-art-studio.jpg` is a crop of PCB Art Studio's live board preview, rendered at high resolution (Save preview) from the tool's own built-in sample artwork, the blue alisoglu.com business card, on 6 November 2026. It shows the tool's preview, not a photograph of a manufactured board.

The pages show `.webp` copies of these screenshots (and of `repodock-dashboard.png`), made with quality 82 at the same size. They are 30–60% smaller. The originals stay for link preview cards and full-size links. After replacing a screenshot, regenerate its `.webp` copy.

`pcb-sunrise-board.webp` is PCB Art Studio's own high-resolution preview (Save preview) of `../pcb-art/sunrise.svg`, a drawing made for the site, on an 80 × 80 mm square tile with blue solder mask and ENIG, rendered on 27 November 2026. The homepage slider compares it with the drawing itself. It shows the tool's preview, not a photograph of a manufactured board.

Since the second pass the homepage slider uses `pcb-sunrise-art.webp` and `pcb-sunrise-board.webp`: the same scene rendered twice in a browser (CSS 3D, studio lighting, desk texture), once with the drawing as a paper print and once with PCB Art Studio's board render including its 1.6 mm edge. Both are renders, not photographs; no board has been manufactured yet.
