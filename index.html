01 / PROCESSING

In your browser

Canvas, browser workers and WebAssembly handle the file work. Background removal and text recognition run their models on your device.

02 / YOUR FILES

No upload step

Your selected files and generated results aren’t sent to a conversion server. The workspace passes them between tools in memory.

03 / NETWORK & SAVING

Website downloads, explained

Your browser requests pages, code and engines from this host, which receives ordinary connection information. Download your result before closing the tab; results aren’t saved here.
