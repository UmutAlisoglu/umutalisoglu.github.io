The idea

Skate Scape combines an open 3D world with flips, grabs, grinds, manuals and trick combinations. Drop obstacles, set a session marker, try challenges and find a new line.

A design choice: room to explore

Free skating sits alongside challenges, so you can choose a goal or just try a line. The game lives in its own repository. It’s a personal fan project, made for the fun of exploring a familiar world on a skateboard.

It is an unofficial fan project. See the project’s credits and notices ↗

BEFORE YOU ROLL

Give the world a moment.

Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer. The first visit downloads about 95 MB from the 2009scape project on GitLab and builds the world on your device. Loading time depends on your connection and computer.

The game caches data locally for later visits and saves progress in the browser. Clearing this site’s browser data can remove both cached game data and progress.
