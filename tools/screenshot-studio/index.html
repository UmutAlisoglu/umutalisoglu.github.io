Show what you made.

    Use a screenshot of your app, a project, a design or a good-looking piece of code. The sample is original artwork generated locally.

  2. Find its frame

    Choose a background, give it some space and add a caption. The preview updates as you change the styling.

    Download the composition in exact landscape, square or widescreen dimensions. Keep editing, or continue with your result in the workspace.

Made for sharing

Great for project announcements, portfolio images, slide decks and social posts. This tool frames a screenshot you choose; it does not capture websites or create an interactive app.

The details

The screenshot is fitted without cropping. It may be resized to fit your export. PNG is lossless; JPG and WebP use 92% quality. Captions fit up to two lines, with an ellipsis if needed. Source metadata is removed.
