You're in control

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Are my pictures sent to an AI service?

No. The model and runtime download from this website; your picture is processed on your device and is not uploaded or used for training.

Does the editor reduce my image size?

No. The preview is scaled to fit your screen, but the cutout mask and PNG export use the original decoded image dimensions. Animated images use the first frame.

Can I come back to my edits later?

Keep this page open until you download. Your image, mask and undo history stay in memory and are cleared when you clear the file or leave the page.

Your files stay with you.

No file upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
