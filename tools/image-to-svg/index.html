Before you start

Output is a traced approximation, not a reconstruction of original design layers, fonts or editable text. Large photos may produce complex shapes. Images are scaled down for predictable browser performance. Transparent areas remain transparent.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Is the original image just embedded inside an SVG?

No. The tool traces pixels into vector paths. It does not wrap the original raster image in an SVG file.

Can it recreate my original logo exactly?

Not always. A raster image has already lost its original vector structure. Review the result, particularly small text and fine lines.

Your files stay with you.

No file upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
