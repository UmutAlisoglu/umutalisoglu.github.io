GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is read and processed in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server, and the result disappears when you close the page, so download it first.

Why did my PDF barely get smaller?

Text, fonts and vector drawings are already compact and are left untouched. Only ordinary RGB JPEG images are recompressed. If a PDF has none, there is little to save.

Will the text stay selectable?

Yes. Text is never turned into an image, and the page layout stays the same.

Why are forms and signed PDFs refused?

Rewriting the file would break a digital signature and can damage form fields. Compress a copy without the signature, or ask the sender for a smaller version.

Your files stay with you.

No upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
