GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Does this upload my documents?

No. The engine and chosen language download from this website. Your image or PDF is read on your device and is not sent to a conversion or AI service.

Can it read scanned PDFs?

Yes. It renders the selected PDF pages locally, then reads their images with OCR. The result is editable plain text, with page separators for multiple pages.

Will it reproduce my document exactly?

No. OCR is an approximation. It can miss or misread letters and will not recreate columns, tables or the original layout. Review the editable output before using it.

What happens when I clear the tool?

The selected file, previews and text are removed from this page and the OCR worker is stopped. The browser may keep public engine downloads in its HTTP or optional offline cache.

Your files stay with you.

No file upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
