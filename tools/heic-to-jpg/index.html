THREE SMALL STEPS

Here’s how it works.

  1. Choose a HEIC photo

    One HEIC or HEIF photo up to 20 MB and 24 megapixels, straight from an iPhone or iPad.

  2. Pick the quality

    Balanced (85%) is a good default. High (95%) keeps more detail for printing or editing.

  3. Convert & download

    You get a standard JPG that opens on Windows, Android and every website.

Before you start

Only the first image is converted. HDR, depth data and the video part of a Live Photo are not kept. Location and camera details are not copied into the JPG.

A file won’t open?

Some HEIC variants are not supported in the browser. Export the photo as JPG from the Photos app, or share it with “Most compatible” turned on.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is read and processed in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server, and the result disappears when you close the page, so download it first.

Does the JPG keep my location?

No. Location and camera metadata are not copied, so the JPG is safer to share.

Can I convert several photos at once?

One photo at a time. Choose the next photo as soon as the first one has downloaded.

Why is the JPG bigger than the HEIC?

HEIC uses a newer, more efficient compression. A JPG of the same photo is usually larger. Image studio can make it smaller afterwards.

Your files stay with you.

No upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
