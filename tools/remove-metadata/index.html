GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is read and processed in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server, and the result disappears when you close the page, so download it first.

Does it reduce image quality?

No. The image data is copied as it is, without recompression. Only the metadata blocks are removed.

Which information is removed?

GPS coordinates, camera make, model and serial number, capture settings, embedded thumbnails, comments and descriptive tags such as author or copyright.

Is the photo now anonymous?

The hidden data is gone, but the picture itself can still reveal a place or a person. Check what is visible before you share it.

Your files stay with you.

No upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
