GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Does cropping change my original file?

No. The tool creates a new download. Your original file is unchanged.

Can I crop without dragging?

Yes. Use the Left, Top, Width and Height fields to choose exact pixel coordinates.

Your files stay with you.

No file upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
