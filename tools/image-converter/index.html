THREE SMALL STEPS

Here’s how it works.

  1. Choose your images

    Drop in files, then use the arrows to set their order. You can add more to the same batch.

  2. Pick the output

    Choose a format and maximum width, or start with a preset. A width of 0 keeps the original size.

  3. Convert & download

    A single image downloads directly. Multiple images become a ZIP; PDF output puts them into one document.

Before you start

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Will my images always get smaller?

No. Size depends on the source, output format and quality. The result shows the difference. Resizing large images usually helps; converting to PNG can make a file larger.

Can I put several images into one PDF?

Yes. Select PDF as the output. Each image gets an A4 page, in the displayed order. Portrait or landscape is chosen to suit the image.

Do you remember my photos?

No. The optional remember checkbox saves only format, quality and width on this device. It never saves files or filenames. Uncheck it to forget those settings.

Your files stay with you.

Selected files are processed in your browser. No upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
