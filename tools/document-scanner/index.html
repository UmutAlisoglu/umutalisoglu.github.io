MAKE THE PAGE THE FOCUS

From desk to document.

  1. Frame the page

    Choose an upright photo with the complete page visible. Drag all four handles to its corners.

  2. Change perspective

    The scanner maps the selected quadrilateral into a rectangle and samples the real image pixels.

  3. Clean & export

    Review the preview, choose a treatment and prepare PNG, JPG or a one-page image PDF.

Start with a clear photo

Perspective correction straightens a flat page. It cannot unfold curved paper, restore blurry letters or remove every shadow. Corner placement is manual; the generated sample starts with its known corners selected.

The output’s shape is estimated from the selected edge lengths. Strong camera angles can affect proportions.
