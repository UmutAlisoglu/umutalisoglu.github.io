THREE SMALL STEPS

Here’s how it works.

  1. Add your documents

    Choose at least two unlocked PDFs. You can keep adding files before merging.

  2. Set their order

    Move documents up or down in the list. Every page from each file is included.

  3. Merge & download

    Get one combined PDF. For individual page choices or rotations, open PDF organizer instead.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Can I reorder individual pages?

Use PDF organizer for individual page previews, rotation and selection. This tool orders whole documents.

Does merging reduce file size?

Merging combines pages; it is not PDF compression. The final size depends on the content of the source PDFs.

Do my original files change?

No. You download a new document. The browser cannot overwrite your originals through this tool.

Your files stay with you.

Selected files are processed in your browser. No upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
