THREE SMALL STEPS

Here’s how it works.

  1. Load your PDFs

    Choose one or more unlocked PDFs and press “Load pages” to see the thumbnails.

  2. Make it yours

    Drag cards or use the arrows, rotate pages, and uncheck anything you want to leave out.

  3. Export the selection

    Download a new PDF with the selected pages in their displayed order. Your source files stay unchanged.

✳

Before you start

1–20 unlocked PDFs, up to 25 MB each and 75 MB total. Maximum 200 pages. Forms, bookmarks and digital signatures may not be preserved. Page omission is not secure redaction.

Try it for…

A scanned document has page three upside down? Load it, rotate that page twice, then export. Need to undo your changes? “Reset pages” restores the original arrangement.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Does this turn my PDF into low-quality images?

No. Thumbnails are only for the preview. Export copies the actual PDF pages, keeping their original page content.

Can I take pages from different PDFs?

Yes. Add the PDFs together, load their pages, and keep only the pages you need. The final order follows the page cards.

What about password-protected PDFs?

Use unlocked copies you are authorised to edit. This tool does not remove passwords. For large documents that only need joining, use Merge PDFs instead.

Your files stay with you.

Selected files are processed in your browser. No upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
