THREE SMALL STEPS

Here’s how it works.

  1. Paste or open text

    Paste up to 200,000 characters or open one UTF-8 TXT file up to 1 MB.

  2. Pick page and font size

    A4 or US Letter, 9 to 24 pt. Long text flows onto new pages automatically.

  3. Create & download

    The PDF keeps real, selectable text and is ready to print or share.

Before you start

Up to 100 pages. Characters the bundled font cannot show are replaced with squares and reported. Formatting such as bold or headings is not applied.

Meeting notes, a quick letter or a printable checklist.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Are my files uploaded or stored?

No. Processing happens in your browser. Files, pasted text and results stay in this tab’s memory. Download the result before leaving. The host receives ordinary page requests, not your selected file contents.

Does “local” mean no internet requests?

The browser downloads this page and its code from this website. Selected files and pasted text aren’t uploaded. Optional offline mode saves public tool code; it doesn’t save your files or results.

Your files stay with you.

Free to use. No upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving.
