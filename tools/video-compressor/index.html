GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Will it always meet my target?

The output size is checked and the encoder can retry. If the target cannot be reached within the available limits, the tool reports a failure. Very small targets can noticeably reduce quality.

Why does it take time?

Your device runs the video encoder itself. Two passes help distribute the available bitrate. Keep the tab open and active; phones and older computers can be much slower.

Does this trim videos or extract audio?

No. This compresses the full video. You can keep or remove its audio track. Video trimming and separate audio downloads are not included.

Your files stay with you.

Selected files are processed in your browser. No upload, account or saved conversion history. Download your result before leaving the page.
